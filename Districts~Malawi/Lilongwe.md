## #has_/text_of_/abstract 

> **Lilongwe** is the capital and largest city of Malawi. 
> It has a population of 989,318 as of the 2018 Census, up from a population of 674,448 in 2008. 
> In 2020, that figure was 1,122,000. The city is located in the central region of Malawi, 
> in the district of the same name, near the borders with Mozambique and Zambia, 
> and it is an important economic and transportation hub for central Malawi. 
> 
> It is named after the Lilongwe River.
>
> [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lilongwe)
